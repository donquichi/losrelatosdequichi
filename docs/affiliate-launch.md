## Program shortlist

| Candidate | Fit to assess | First decision |
| --- | --- | --- |

Recommendation: assess Amazon Associates US first for an initial three to five useful recommendations, while retaining Awin as an alternative if suitable merchants or audience destinations make it a better fit. This is a proposed starting point, not an instruction to register with multiple networks or a prediction of earnings.

Before applying, review the actual site's content against Amazon's [participation requirements](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies) and [application review guidance](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G8TW5AE9XL2VX9VM). Acceptance is not established by having an accessible website. The US review guidance calls for at least three qualifying sales within the first 180 days, excludes personal orders, and reviews declared sites and recent substantive content. Recheck the current guidance when registering rather than treating these notes as permanent requirements.

The owner completes sign-in, personal account/payment/tax information, and acceptance of program agreements directly on the provider's website. Keep those details private. No account or payment enrollment is performed by this document.

## First Recomendados page specification

- Preserve the black background, existing red accents, typography, and card proportions.
- Begin with three to five editorially reviewed recommendations, prioritizing books relevant to the stories. Products remain undecided; do not present unreviewed products as personally tested.
- Each item needs its title, a short genuine reason for inclusion, an appropriate category, approved image if available, actual merchant destination, and an affiliate link generated under the owner's account.
- Use “Ver en Amazon” or an equivalent merchant-specific action, rather than implying Los Relatos de Quichi handles checkout.
- Show clear affiliate identification before the links and use the provider's required wording where applicable. Amazon's [agreement](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/agreement) requires identifying the site as an Associate. Do not label affiliate purchases as donations or ask readers to support the project by using Amazon links; its application guidance prohibits that type of request.
- Keep any donation invitation separate. Do not copy changing prices, ratings, or merchant product images without confirming the permitted source and display method.
- Use lightweight cards and normal links with sponsored-link markup. Avoid adding a tracking widget simply to render products.
- On phones, use a single readable column and generous link targets; on desktop, reuse a compact grid compatible with the existing theme.
- Add the navigation entry only once the page has valid content and links. Do not deploy an empty recommendations page, fabricated referral tags, or placeholder buy buttons.
